Nameless

«بلا اسم» · Android · by Ahmed Ramadan Tanany

Open a WhatsApp chat with any number you type — without saving that number to your contacts. No account, no server of ours, nothing uploaded.

  • Type a number, tap once, the chat opens in WhatsApp.
  • Save a number to the app's private list only when you want to.
  • Arabic and English, light and dark.
